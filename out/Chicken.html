Class: Chicken

Chicken(imagePath, width, height, x, y, speed, energy, damagePoints, damageTime)

Represents a normal walking enemy chicken. Inherits movement and animation logic from MovableObject.

Constructor

new Chicken(imagePath, width, height, x, y, speed, energy, damagePoints, damageTime)

Initializes a Chicken instance with random position and speed.
